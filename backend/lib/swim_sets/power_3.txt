Pre Set

Main Set
Beat the Clock (~1750 yards)
Format: Swimmer starts on the top of the clock and kicks 50
yards.  The swimmer must be back before the 59, 58, 57... but 
they still always leave at the top of the clock.  Once the swimmer
isn't able to make the interval anymore, they start swimming, after
they cant make it anymore they put on fins.  Once they aren'the
able to make the interval with fins, the set is over.

Cool Down
200 Free Smooth
